      What is Love Blueprint?

      Love Blueprint is a free dating-profile reading built from your exact birth chart. Instead of a generic horoscope, it computes your real planetary positions, Venus, Moon, Mercury, Mars, your Ascendant and 7th House and translates them into the things you actually need on a dating app: how you love, how you text, what kind of first date suits you, which red flags to watch for and a profile bio you can copy straight into Hinge, Bumble or Tinder.

      How the calculations work

      Every reading on this page is computed in your browser, the moment you press “Reveal My Blueprint”, using a real astronomical ephemeris (the open-source astronomy-engine library) and exact sidereal-time formulas for the Ascendant and houses. Your birth place is resolved against a 14,133-city dataset with proper IANA time zones, so daylight-saving birthdays are handled correctly.

      We also hold an accuracy line most sites don’t: anything that can’t be computed exactly from what you gave us is withheld, not estimated. No birth time? Your Rising sign and 7th House stay locked with a plain explanation, because guessing them would be inventing your compatibility profile.

      Compatibility, without surrendering your privacy

      You can share a “blueprint” link or code that contains only your finished readings : never your birth date, time or place. The recipient sees what you chose to show, saves you to their potential matches and can compare sparks. For the deepest reading (exact degree synastry with real aspect orbs, plus a composite chart), both charts are entered on one device, computed and discarded: degrees are never stored or transmitted, because a full set of degrees could be used to fingerprint a birth chart.

      What Love Blueprint is not

      • Not a prediction engine. Nothing here tells you whom you will marry or when. Astrology is a reflective language; we treat it that way.
      • Not a verdict on any person. Low “ease” scores describe friction that many lasting couples work through happily. Two humans are more than their degrees.
      • Not a data collector. There is no account, no upload, no server-side storage of anything you enter. Your library (saved readings, match notes) lives in this browser’s local storage and disappears if you clear it.
      • Not personalised advice. For relationship decisions, talk to the humans in your life or a qualified counsellor.

      The features

      • Love Signature : seven flip cards: Venus (how you love), Moon (emotional needs), Mercury (how you text), Mars (chemistry), Rising (first-date vibe), 7th House (who you’re wired for), Sun (your dating superpower).
      • Your Words vs Your Chart. You choose what you’re here for; we show (without judging) whether that matches your chart’s pull.
      • The Deeper Pull. Your North/South Node axis and Saturn contacts: what’s comfortable versus what stretches you.
      • Bio Writer : three copy-paste bios composed from your real placements, in your tone, editable before you post.
      • Degree synastry : exact aspects between two charts with measurable orbs, honest CONTACT/EASE axes and a composite chart.
